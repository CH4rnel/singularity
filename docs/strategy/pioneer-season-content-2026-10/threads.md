## T2 · Wed Oct 28 · "Creator fees, in public" · gate G2

1/ CYBER.sol pays its creator a fee on every trade on PumpSwap. That's the only money Cyberia's growth spends: no VC, no presale, no team sell-off. Here's how we'll use it. 🧵

—

2/ The rate: 0.30% of volume below 420 SOL market cap and 0.95% above it (pump.fun's own fee table). At today's volume that's [[FILL: SOL/week from the fee wallet]], so it's real money, but small.

—

3/ The rule:
• nothing is spent until 0.10 SOL has accumulated
• then each week 60% goes to bounties and growth, up to 20% to infrastructure, and at least 20% to reserve
• unspent infrastructure money rolls into the reserve

—

4/ What fees will never pay for:
• buying CYBER.sol to move the chart
• paid "calls" or undisclosed promotion
• rewards for trading volume, follows or reposts

—

5/ What they do pay for: Pioneer bounties. Reproduced bugs, translations, guides, demos, integrations, token sites. Real work, reviewed in public, paid in SOL.

—

6/ The ledger, with received, spent, on what and the tx link, is pinned in t.me/cyberia_network_chat and updated every Monday. If a number there is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it in public.
