# Explore the Cyberia Ecosystem

Once your wallet is connected to Cyberia and holds a little CYBER for gas, you can use the rest of the ecosystem from <https://cyberia.church>.

## Before any on-chain action

1. Confirm that the wallet shows **Cyberia** and chain ID `49406`.
2. Confirm that the connected address is the one you intend to use.
3. Read the asset, amount, destination, and fee shown by the page.
4. Review the wallet confirmation before signing.
5. Save the transaction hash and check it in the explorer.

## Supply or borrow in lending

Open <https://cyberia.church/lending>.

1. Connect your EVM wallet on Cyberia.
2. Choose the asset market you want to use.
3. To supply, enter an amount, approve the ERC-20 when requested, and confirm the supply transaction.
4. To use supplied assets as collateral, enable the market through the interface and confirm the wallet action.
5. To borrow, choose an available asset and an amount within the displayed account limits.
6. Follow the position summary and health information on the page.
7. Repay a borrow or withdraw supplied assets from the same market screen.

Each approval and market action is a separate transaction and therefore has its own hash.

## Stake or farm

Open <https://cyberia.church/staking> for token staking or <https://cyberia.church/farm> for LP-token farms.

1. Choose a listed pool and read its deposit asset and reward asset.
2. If a farm requires LP tokens, first add the matching pair on the [liquidity page](dex.md#providing-liquidity).
3. Enter the amount to stake.
4. Approve the token if requested, then confirm the stake.
5. Return to the pool to view the position, claim available rewards, or unstake.

Pool terms are displayed by the live interface, so use the values shown at the time of your action.

## Launch a community token

Open <https://cyberia.church/launchpad>.

1. Connect a Cyberia wallet that has CYBER for the launch and network fee.
2. Enter the token name, symbol, description, and the available media or links you want to publish.
3. Review the supply and CYBER liquidity shown by the launch form.
4. Confirm the summary and submit the launch transaction.
5. Open the new token page and its explorer link after confirmation.
6. Share the token page or its custom launchpad site.

The launch transaction creates the token and its initial CYBER liquidity according to the parameters displayed by the page.

## Join a DAO

Open <https://cyberia.church/dao>.

1. Choose a DAO from the directory.
2. Read its description, membership rules, proposals, and voting state.
3. Open a proposal to review its requested action and voting period.
4. Connect the eligible wallet and choose the available voting action.
5. Confirm the transaction and verify it through the proposal page or explorer.

Proposal creation is available from the DAO interface when the connected account meets that DAO's rules.

## Use the NFT market

Open <https://cyberia.church/market>.

1. Connect your Cyberia wallet.
2. Browse an NFT and open its detail view.
3. Review its contract, token identifier, owner, and listed amount.
4. Choose the available market action and confirm it in the wallet.
5. Verify the ownership or listing update after the transaction confirms.

## Play on-chain games

Cyberia includes [Slots](https://cyberia.church/slots), [Predictions](https://cyberia.church/predictions), and [Pixel Battle](https://cyberia.church/pixels).

1. Open a game and read the current round or action rules.
2. Connect a wallet on Cyberia.
3. Enter the amount or choose the board action offered by the game.
4. Review the CYBER amount and gas fee.
5. Confirm the transaction and follow the result on the page.

## Explore public data

- <https://cyberia.church/tokens> lists Cyberia tokens and their market pages.
- <https://cyberia.church/analytics> presents public network, pool, and activity figures.
- <https://explorer.cyberia.church> shows blocks, transactions, addresses, tokens, and contracts.
- <https://cyberia.church/profile> brings together attached wallets, nickname, progression, and achievements.

If you are unsure what a wallet prompt means, pause at the confirmation screen and compare it with [Crypto basics](crypto-basics.md) before signing.
